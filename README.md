We propose a new nature inspired metaheuristic approach based on the V flight formation
of the migrating birds which is proven to be an effective formation in energy saving. Its performance
is tested on quadratic assignment problem instances arising from a real life problem
and very good results are obtained. The quality of the solutions we report are better
than simulated annealing, tabu search, genetic algorithm, scatter search, particle swarm
optimization, differential evolution and guided evolutionary simulated annealing
approaches. The proposed method is also tested on a number of benchmark problems
obtained from the QAPLIB and in most cases it was able to obtain the best known solutions.
These results indicate that our new metaheuristic approach could be an important player
in metaheuristic based optimization.
